UI/UX Design — Group Project

Shopee UGC Task Module

Feature concept & prototype, with Wong Siew Kei and Lim Mei Hui

A proposed feature module for Shopee that turns everyday shoppers into active content creators. Young users (18–24) are Shopee's core audience but show little brand loyalty, and platforms still lean on discounts and KOLs rather than the shoppers themselves. This concept — "Bought It? Say It!" — reframes reviews as weekly themed tasks with real rewards, turning a passive purchase into an ongoing, community-driven habit.

About the Project

Problem

Young users drive Shopee's traffic but rarely stay loyal. User-generated content is proven to influence purchases more than KOLs, yet current platforms mostly reward watching videos or leaving one-off comments — there's no structured incentive to keep creating.

Research

A literature review of Shopee, TikTok and Lazada (AI recommendations, shoppertainment, influencer marketing, UGC trends), paired with a survey of 25 users aged 18–24 about their shopping habits and what would motivate them to post more.

Solution

A UGC Task Module: weekly themed tasks that reward photo/video reviews with Shopee Coins, coupons and badges — surfaced through a Task Wall, a personal Task Center, and a creator leaderboard for recognition.

Proposed Solution — "Bought It? Say It!"
01
Weekly themed tasks

e.g. "Home Goodies" — a fresh content prompt every week to keep participation going.

02
Photo/video + comment

Upload unboxing photos or videos with a short creative caption, not just a star rating.

03
Real rewards

Shopee Coins, coupons and badges for every completed task, tracked in a personal center.

04
Featured content

Strong submissions get surfaced on product pages and the Task Wall itself.

05
Task Wall & Creator List

A shared space to browse tasks, see other creators' entries, and follow favorites.

06
Leaderboard recognition

Weekly rankings turn consistent creators into recognized top reviewers.

User Flow
1

Post-checkout invite

Right after an order is placed, a pop-up invites the shopper to join a task for that purchase — catching them at the moment they're most engaged with the product.

Confirmation doubles as the task invite
2

Pick a task

The Task Wall centers every weekly and daily task in one place, each with its own reward, deadline and submission type — text, photo or video.

Weekly / Daily filters
3

Submit & rate

Users upload a photo or video of the product, rate their experience, and see exactly how many coins and points the submission is worth before confirming.

Tap to rate, reward shown live
4

Track & redeem

A personal Task Center holds the point history and progress toward the next reward, while My Rewards lets users redeem points for vouchers and coupons any time.

Live point balance
5

Get recognized

A weekly Leaderboard and Top Reviewer picks turn consistent creators into recognized names — and their reviews feed back into product pages other shoppers browse.

Top 3 highlighted weekly
Prototype Screens
Style

Visual language

Built on Shopee's own orange-and-white system so the module feels native to the app rather than bolted on — clean cards, rounded task chips, and high-contrast CTAs.

Tone

Playful and rewarding rather than transactional: coins, badges and progress bars keep the focus on participation and community, not just discounts.

Prototype

Built and tested as an interactive Figma prototype covering the full loop from checkout to submission to redemption.
