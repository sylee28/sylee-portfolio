About the Project

Concept

A fan-made chibi comic collection based on Meridian Project — turning six VTubers' personalities into short, funny, one-page stories that longtime fans and newcomers can both enjoy.

Process

Concept development and character research, then short scripts, one-page chibi comics, character supplement pages, and two bonus episodes — 36 pages in total, fully bilingual.

Output

A complete fan product: the printed & digital comic book, plus a matching merch line of postcards, stickers, acrylic standees and keychains.

Character Showcase

NEO(n) — Rei, Yuzumi & Kirali, Meridian Project's beloved trio — alongside Seki, Hibiki and KSP, three very different personalities who keep every story unpredictable.
