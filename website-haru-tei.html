Website Design — Group Project

Haru Tei 春亭

Content Management module project, with Wong Siew Kei

A Japanese ramen restaurant founded by Vincent Teoh in 2015, planning to grow into a chain of outlets across Malaysia. The brief called for a high-fidelity, e-commerce-ready website, researched against comparable restaurant sites, then built and developed in WordPress.

Research & Analysis

History

A Japanese ramen restaurant founded by Vincent Teoh in 2015.

Vision

To have more outlets.

Mission

To be able to have a chain of restaurants all over Malaysia.

Achievement

Mockups serve as a blueprint by outlining core interactions and concepts before production.

Website Development

Brief

Our client is planning to have an e-commerce website, so the design needed to read as polished and shop-ready, not just a brochure site.

Typography

Set entirely in Inter, for a clean, highly legible sans-serif that stays neutral against the restaurant's warm photography.

Build

Designed as a high-fidelity Figma prototype, then built and published live on WordPress.
